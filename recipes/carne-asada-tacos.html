Carne Asada Tacos

Description

Carne asada tacos are a popular Mexican street taco made with tender, marinated, and grilled beef topped with onion and cilantro all wrapped in a corn tortilla.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb skirt or flank steak
  • 1 bunch of cilantro
  • 1 white onion
  • 4 limes
  • 3 tbsp Lawry's seasoned salt (or replace with your seasoning salt of choice)

Steps

  1. Remove onion's tunic and cut in half towards the root.
  2. Place each half with their inside faced into the cutting board and cut a small portion to remove nose of the onion.
  3. Dice the onion.
  4. Mince the cilantro.
  5. Place onion and cilantro into a bowl and mix.
  6. Slice all limes into halves.
  7. Dice the taco meat.
  8. Place taco meat into a bowl.
  9. Squeeze four lime halves into the bowl with the meat.
  10. Add seasoning salt into bowl with the meat then hand mix.
  11. Place meat into a pan and cook on med-high heat. Be sure to occasionally stir. Assuming meat is diced, the meat is cooked when dark brown.
  12. In another pan, warm up corn tortillas on low-med heat. Be sure to flip for even heating and to prevent burning.
  13. Place a line of meat across the diameter of the tortilla.
  14. Garnish the taco with the onion and cilantro mixture.
  15. Squeeze some fresh lime and sprinkle a little table salt over the taco.
  16. Enjoy!
